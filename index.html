The Quality Ladder

Not all “Raw” is Raw.

The market runs on very different products sold under similar names. Knowing the difference is the whole game — for your chair, your client and your reputation.

✓The real luxury

Virgin Remy

One donor, completely unprocessed and healthy hair. Cuticles healthy, intact and naturally flowing in the same direction, root-to-tip. As it comes from a single full head shaved (not cut or trimmed), it is inherently single-drawn — uniform in texture and health through the whole bundle.

~Good, with caveats — misrepresented as Raw/Virgin

Remy

Cuticles still aligned and running one direction — but the source can be the discards of virgin Remy, donors whose hair isn’t healthy, or hair cut from the donor (not root-to-tip, so it won’t make the weight for a 100 g / 3.5 oz bundle), among others. Because the hair is blended, it’s treated with milder chemicals for a uniform texture and can be offered double or super-double drawn. Good hair — but a major step down from virgin, and often falsely labelled to sell.

✕Mislabeled as Raw/Virgin

Machine Remy

Collected shed hair, pooled from many people, with cuticles mechanically realigned to imitate Remy and mildly stripped for smoothness and shine. Frequently mislabelled “raw unprocessed.” It’s a dead-hair strand — it was never in the same league.

✕The base tier

Non-Remy

Collected shed hair, pooled from many people, with cuticles chemically stripped for a smooth, shiny finish. With machine Remy taking over for about a decade, this form is largely no longer sold to the West as a finished product.

  • ✓Single-donor uniformity — texture and health stay consistent through the entire bundle.
  • ✓Cuticles intact & aligned root-to-tip — not stripped and re-laid by machine.
  • ✓Natural, un-dyed colour with subtle gradation toward the ends.
  • ✓Honest origin — a country’s population has to actually explain its “raw” export volume.
  • ✓Single-drawn variation — true virgin Remy carries a natural range of lengths.
  • ✓Yield-honest grading — a full-weight bundle that is genuinely single donor, not pooled from many to make the weight.
